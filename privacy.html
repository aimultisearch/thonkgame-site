Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

THONK! is a mobile game made by LM Tech Studio, Geneva, Switzerland. This page explains what data the game uses and why. The short version: we don't ask for your name, email or any account, and your progress stays on your phone. Ads, in-app purchases and the leaderboard are run by Google, under Google's own policies.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, hammers, items, settings and records) is saved in a file on your device. We don't receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it, unless you use cloud save (see below).

Ads (Google AdMob)

THONK! shows ads through Google AdMob: optional reward videos (for example, to double your coins or get a revive) and occasional ads between levels. To do this, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and basic device information;
  • how you interact with ads and the app;
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ads are loaded, through Google's consent form. You can refuse: you will still see ads, but not personalized ones. You can change your choice at any time from the game's options. More information: how Google uses data in ads.

Buying "No Ads" removes the ads between levels. Reward videos stay available, because they are always your choice.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so the game can give you what you bought and restore it on a new device.

Leaderboard and cloud save (Google Play Games)

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. If you do, Google Play Games uses your Play Games profile to show your Endless score on the world leaderboard and, if enabled, to save your progress in the cloud. The nickname you choose for Endless is shown to other players on the leaderboard. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

THONK! is a cartoon game that anyone can enjoy, but it is not made specifically for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served through Google's certified ad network and follow Google Play's Families policies where they apply.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • There is no chat and no content shared between players, apart from the leaderboard nickname.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent from the game's options.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Delete your local data by uninstalling the game. To delete Play Games data, manage it from your Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
